Method

How the maps are made

01

Map the work as practitioners do it

Every step, handoff and check, including the ones nobody writes down.

02

Anchor it to the standard

Each task cites the published standard that speaks to it, or is marked as practitioner practice. An independent check verifies every citation.

03

Name an owner and classify

Every task and gate has an owner. Each step is tagged for what a model can run, what it drafts for sign-off, and what stays with people.

04

Write it for an agent

Turn the map into a skills file, then check whether a model actually follows it.

Caesar Rana is a CFA charterholder, an APMG Certified PPP Professional and a former IFC Operations Officer. The maps were built at SpaceXAI and are shared with its permission. They describe general methods and contain no client data.
