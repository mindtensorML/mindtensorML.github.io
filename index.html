Process maps for AI in investment work

Caesar Rana, CFA

These maps show how investment work actually gets done, step by step. For each step I decide whether a model can do it alone, whether a person should review it, or whether it has to stay with people. Each map is written so an AI agent can follow it as a skills file.

I built them at SpaceXAI, where I lead the portfolio management pod, and they are shared here with SpaceXAI's permission. Together they cover more than 500 mapped steps and tasks.

The EM infrastructure portfolio map was turned into a skills file that a frontier model followed through most of the process. That design is now the basis of an ongoing SpaceXAI project.

How the maps are made

  1. Map the work as practitioners do it. Every step, handoff and check, including the ones nobody writes down.
  2. Classify each step. Decide what a model can do alone, what needs a reviewer, and what stays with people.
  3. Put the gates where the risk is. Go or no-go points sit before money or reputation is committed.
  4. Write it for an agent. Turn the map into a skills file, then check whether a model actually follows it.
